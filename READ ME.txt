UPLOAD TO GITHUB
================

Upload BOTH files to your repository. That is the whole website.

    index.html
    rules.html

No assets folder and no .nojekyll needed - every image is embedded.

  In your repo:  Add file -> Upload files
  Drag both in, Commit changes
  Wait 30-60 seconds, then hard-refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R / Cmd+Shift+R)

  Your site:  https://jpd-coder.github.io/FLJAC/
  Rules page: /rules.html

Do not open these in GitHub's built-in editor - the embedded images make
the lines very long and the editor struggles. Uploading is fine.
