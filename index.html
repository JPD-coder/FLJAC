GAME 1

The Club League

Every game at Dania Beach Jai Alai has eight entries standing at posts 1 through 8. Each session, each club is assigned a post — whoever stands there all night is playing for your club. You don't need to know a single player's name. You just need to watch and root for your favorite team.

Free to enter · Unlimited entries · $15,000 in prizes

Results are posted the day after each card. Standings run on a one-day lag all season — nothing updates live during play.

How to enter

  • Pick your club. Any of the eight. No caps, no limits — if 800 people want the Square Groupers, that's 800 Square Groupers fans.
  • Predict wins, places and shows. Every club lands near 115 of each across the season. The club that wins the season usually records about 130 wins — and slightly fewer places than average, because a game it wins can't also be one it places in. Closest win count takes the title; places then shows break any tie.

It's a dead-even race, which means it's genuinely undecided in March. Before entering you'll be asked to read and accept the rules.

GAME 2

The Player League

Pick six players from Dania Beach Jai Alai's 22-man roster and ride them for two months. No weekly homework, no lineup to reset every Tuesday. Set it and watch.

$100 to enter · $85,000 in prizes · Feb 2 – Mar 31

Your lineup

  • Six players, minimum two frontcourt and minimum two backcourt.
  • Every player has a price and you have a fixed budget. You cannot afford the six best — that's the whole game.
  • Name one captain, who scores 1.5×.

How you score

Your players earn points all season from real results: win (1st), place (2nd) and show (3rd). Nothing below third. Every game counts, singles and doubles.

Results are posted the day after each card — Tuesday's matinee and evening both land on Wednesday. There is no live in-game scoring.

When you're handicapping, watch usage as closely as talent. Evenings are 63% of the card, so a player who only works matinees gets around 120 starts while someone working both sessions gets close to 330. Volume beats rate.

Three weeks to do your homework

The season opens January 5 but nothing counts for the first three weeks. You watch, you study, and so does everyone else.

Seven of the 22 players are brand new this year with no history anywhere. By late January you'll have seen every one of them play roughly 120 times.

Free to enter

No purchase or payment of any kind is necessary to enter or to win. A purchase will not improve your chance of winning. Void where prohibited.

Who can enter

Aged 18 or over, legal residents of the United States. Employees and immediate family of the Administrator, of Dania Beach Jai Alai, and of any sponsor are not eligible, nor are athletes on the 2027 roster or fronton officials.

Your entry

One entry per person, verified by email link — your entry is not active until you click it. You pick one of the eight clubs, predict its win total between 60 and 160, and give a tiebreaker figure. Entries close 11:59pm ET on January 4, 2027.

How it is decided

Clubs rotate through all eight posts and each plays all 903 games, so no club holds an advantage. The Club League Champion is whoever, among those who picked the winning club, predicted its win total closest — $10,000 and the Championship Belt. The Biggest Fan is the highest fan-points total across the whole field regardless of club — $5,000 and the Biggest Fan Championship Belt.
